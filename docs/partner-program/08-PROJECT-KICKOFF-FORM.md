# Cortex Partner Program — Project Kickoff

**Version:** 1.0  
**Availability:** Only after the project is WON  
**Location:** Inside the relevant project

## Purpose

The Project Kickoff collects the detailed information Cortex needs to begin development.

This is deliberately later in the workflow.

The Partner should NOT be required to provide all of this information during application or initial opportunity submission.

## 1. Business Information

Collect:

- Business name
- Business description
- Relevant business information required for the project

## 2. Branding

Collect:

- Logo
- Primary brand colour
- Secondary brand colour
- Other brand colours where relevant
- Brand guidelines where available

## 3. Media

Allow uploads for:

- Business photos
- Product photos
- Team photos
- Other relevant images

## 4. Content

Collect applicable:

- About/business description
- Services
- Products
- Contact information
- Social media links
- Other approved website/application content

## 5. Pages / Screens

Collect the pages, sections, or screens agreed for the project.

## 6. Features

Collect/confirm the agreed features.

The kickoff must remain consistent with the approved project scope.

## 7. Integrations

Collect applicable integrations, such as:

- Payment
- Email
- SMS
- WhatsApp
- Maps
- External APIs
- Other approved integrations

## 8. Domain / Hosting

Collect:

- Domain information
- Hosting status
- Other non-sensitive deployment information required for development

Do not request passwords or sensitive credentials through ordinary fields.

## 9. Design References

Allow:

- Reference website URLs
- Design notes
- Visual references

## 10. Project Files

Allow appropriate project materials such as:

- PDFs
- Documents
- Catalogues
- Other relevant files

## 11. Submission

After submission, Cortex reviews the kickoff information.

The platform should indicate whether:

- Information is complete
- Clarification is required
- The project is ready for development

## Important Rule

The kickoff becomes available only after the Partner has won the project.

This is where the detailed logo, colour, picture, content, and development information is collected.
