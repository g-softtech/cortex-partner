# Cortex Partner Guide

**Version:** 1.0

## Welcome to the Cortex Partner Program

Cortex provides the technical development capability behind your software opportunities.

You focus on finding opportunities, understanding your customer's needs, managing your customer relationship, and delivering the completed solution.

Cortex handles the technical development behind the scenes.

## How It Works

### 1. Find an Opportunity

Look for businesses that need:

- Websites
- Web applications
- Business systems
- E-commerce solutions
- Custom software
- Digital platforms
- Other approved digital solutions

### 2. Submit the Opportunity

Use the Partner Dashboard and select:

**Submit Project**

Give Cortex enough information to understand what the customer needs.

You do not need to provide all customer branding, files, content, or technical credentials at this stage.

### 3. Cortex Assesses It

Cortex reviews the opportunity and prepares the proposed solution, scope, Partner price, and estimated timeline.

### 4. Work With Your Customer

Use the assessment to discuss the project with your customer.

You remain responsible for the customer relationship.

### 5. Win the Project

Once your customer agrees to proceed, update the project according to the platform workflow.

### 6. Complete Kickoff

The platform will then request the detailed information Cortex needs.

This is where you provide:

- Logo
- Colours
- Images
- Content
- Pages
- Features
- Integrations
- Domain information
- Design references
- Other project materials

### 7. Development

Cortex develops the agreed solution.

### 8. Review

When the project is ready, review it through the Partner Platform.

### 9. Changes

If something needs to change, submit a Change Request.

Do not simply send scattered change instructions through unrelated channels when the project workflow provides a Change Request.

### 10. Delivery

When you are satisfied that the project is ready, use the Delivery Approval process.

You then deliver the solution to your customer.

### 11. Support

For technical issues or assistance, use the Support section.

## What Not to Promise

Do not promise a customer:

- An unapproved feature
- An unapproved timeline
- An unapproved price
- Unlimited revisions
- Services that Cortex has not agreed to provide

When unsure, ask Cortex before making a commitment.

## Important Principle

The Partner Program is designed to make you stronger commercially without requiring you to become a software developer.

**You find the opportunity.  
You manage the relationship.  
Cortex builds the solution.  
You deliver it.**
