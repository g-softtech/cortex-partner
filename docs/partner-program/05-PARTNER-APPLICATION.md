# Cortex Partner Program — Partner Application

**Version:** 1.0  
**Route:** `/apply`  
**Audience:** Public applicants

## Purpose

This form is the first step for a person who wants to become a Cortex Partner.

It is intentionally short. It is not a customer/project intake form.

## Applicant Information

Collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone/WhatsApp number
- Occupation or primary professional role
- What best describes your current work?
- Do you currently know businesses or organizations that may need software or digital solutions?
- If yes, what type of businesses or organizations?
- Why are you interested in becoming a Cortex Partner?
- How did you hear about the Cortex Partner Program?

## Do Not Collect at This Stage

Do not require:

- Customer name
- Customer phone number
- Customer email
- Customer address
- Customer credentials
- Customer logo
- Customer files
- Customer passwords
- Detailed customer requirements

## Submission

After submission:

`PENDING`

The application is reviewed by Cortex.

Possible statuses:

- PENDING
- APPROVED
- MORE_INFORMATION
- DECLINED

An approved applicant proceeds to Partner account setup and agreement acceptance.
