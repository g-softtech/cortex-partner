# Cortex Partner Program — Business Model

**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Business Concept

The Cortex Partner Program enables people and firms to identify software and digital-product opportunities and work with The Cortex Systems as their behind-the-scenes technical development partner.

The Partner focuses on finding opportunities, understanding the customer's needs, managing the customer relationship, and delivering the completed solution.

Cortex focuses on the technical development and delivery of the agreed software solution.

## 2. The Basic Model

**Partner finds the opportunity.**  
↓  
**Partner submits the opportunity to Cortex.**  
↓  
**Cortex assesses the requirements.**  
↓  
**Cortex provides the Partner with the project assessment/proposal and Partner pricing.**  
↓  
**Partner works with the customer and wins the project.**  
↓  
**Partner provides the detailed project information through the kickoff process.**  
↓  
**Cortex develops the solution behind the scenes.**  
↓  
**Partner reviews the completed solution.**  
↓  
**Partner delivers the solution to the customer.**

## 3. Partner's Role

The Partner is primarily responsible for:

- Identifying potential customers and opportunities.
- Building and maintaining the customer relationship.
- Understanding and communicating the customer's business needs.
- Submitting suitable opportunities to Cortex.
- Discussing the proposed solution and commercial arrangement with the customer.
- Providing approved project information to Cortex.
- Coordinating customer feedback through the Partner workflow.
- Reviewing completed work.
- Delivering the completed solution to the customer.

## 4. Cortex's Role

Cortex is primarily responsible for:

- Assessing submitted software opportunities.
- Defining the technical solution and agreed scope.
- Providing development estimates/pricing to the Partner.
- Developing the agreed software or website.
- Handling technical development activities.
- Reviewing and processing technical change requests.
- Providing technical support according to the agreed project/support process.
- Preparing completed work for Partner review and delivery.

## 5. White-Label Relationship

The relationship is designed to allow the Partner to deliver software solutions to their customers while Cortex works behind the scenes as the technical development partner.

The Partner remains the customer's primary relationship owner unless a specific arrangement says otherwise.

Cortex should not unnecessarily interfere with the Partner's customer relationship.

## 6. No Guaranteed Income

The Partner Program does not guarantee customers, projects, revenue, or earnings.

Partner results depend on the Partner's ability to identify opportunities, acquire customers, sell projects, and manage relationships.

## 7. Project-by-Project Model

A person becoming a Partner does not automatically create a development obligation for Cortex.

Each project is separately submitted, assessed, scoped, and accepted through the Partner workflow.

## 8. Scope Control

The agreed project scope determines what Cortex is expected to develop.

Requests outside the agreed scope may require additional assessment, pricing, time, or approval.

## 9. Customer Information Timing

The initial Partner application does not require customer information.

A project opportunity submission captures enough information for Cortex to assess the opportunity.

Detailed business, branding, content, media, technical, domain, and integration information is collected during Project Kickoff after the Partner has won the project.

## 10. Program Principle

The program is designed to create a simple division of responsibility:

**Partner:** opportunity + customer relationship + delivery  
**Cortex:** technical development + technical support

Specific commercial and legal terms are governed by the approved Partner Agreement and applicable project documents.
