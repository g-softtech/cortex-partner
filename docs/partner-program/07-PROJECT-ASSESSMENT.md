# Cortex Partner Program — Project Assessment / Proposal

**Version:** 1.0  
**Location:** Inside the relevant project

## Purpose

The Project Assessment is Cortex's response to a Partner's submitted opportunity.

It gives the Partner the information needed to work with the customer.

## Assessment Contents

### Proposed Solution

Describe the proposed software/digital solution.

### Scope

Clearly identify what is included in the proposed project.

### Included Features

List the major agreed features.

### Exclusions

Where necessary, identify important items that are not included.

### Partner Price

Display the applicable price/charge provided to the Partner.

Do not expose Cortex internal costs, internal margins, internal notes, or internal assessment information to the Partner's customer.

### Estimated Timeline

Provide the estimated development timeline.

### Conditions

Include relevant project conditions.

### Next Step

Clearly tell the Partner what action is required next.

## Principle

The assessment is project-specific.

A submitted opportunity does not automatically mean Cortex has accepted the project for development.

The assessment becomes the basis for the Partner's customer discussion and the subsequent project workflow.
