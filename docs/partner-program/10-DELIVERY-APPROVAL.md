# Cortex Partner Program — Delivery Approval

**Version:** 1.0  
**Location:** Inside the relevant project

## Purpose

Delivery Approval records the Partner's confirmation that the completed project has been reviewed and is ready for delivery to the customer.

## Partner Confirmation

Display:

> I confirm that I have reviewed the completed project and it is ready for delivery to my customer.

Action:

**Approve & Proceed to Delivery**

## Record

The platform should record:

- Partner
- Project
- Approval status
- Date/time
- Relevant project/release version where applicable

## Principle

The Partner remains responsible for customer-facing delivery unless the project arrangement states otherwise.

Delivery Approval should only be available at the appropriate project stage.

The implementation must follow the existing production review/status workflow.
