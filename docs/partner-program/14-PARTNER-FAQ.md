# Cortex Partner Program — FAQ

**Version:** 1.0

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. The Partner's primary role is to identify opportunities, work with customers, and manage the customer relationship. Cortex handles the technical development.

## Do I need to have customers already?

Not necessarily. However, the ability to identify businesses with genuine software needs is important to succeeding as a Partner.

## Who finds the customer?

The Partner finds and develops the customer opportunity.

## Who builds the software?

Cortex handles the agreed technical development.

## Who talks to the customer?

The Partner normally manages the customer relationship. Cortex may communicate directly when reasonably necessary for technical, security, support, legal, payment, or other agreed reasons.

## When do I submit a project?

When you have identified a genuine software or digital-solution opportunity that is suitable for assessment.

## Do I need the customer's full details when I first submit an opportunity?

No. The initial Project Opportunity submission is for assessment. Detailed development information is collected later if the project is won.

## When do I provide the logo, colours and pictures?

During Project Kickoff, after the project has been won.

## What happens after I submit a project?

Cortex reviews the opportunity and may provide a Project Assessment/Proposal containing the proposed solution, scope, Partner price and estimated timeline.

## Does submitting a project guarantee Cortex will build it?

No. Each project is separately assessed and accepted.

## What happens if my customer asks for something outside the original scope?

Submit a Change Request where appropriate. Cortex will assess whether the request is within scope or requires additional work.

## How do I get technical support?

Use the Support section of the Partner Platform.

## Can I earn money from projects?

The Partner may earn a commercial margin according to the applicable project arrangement. The program does not guarantee customers, projects, revenue, or earnings.

## Do I have to tell my customer about Cortex?

The intended relationship is behind-the-scenes/white-label technical development. Follow the Partner Agreement and applicable project terms regarding representation and disclosure.

## Where are the forms?

Operational forms appear inside the relevant workflow.

Resources contain reusable materials such as the Partner Guide, Sales Kit, White-Label Guidelines and FAQ.

## What is the basic model?

**You find the opportunity.  
You manage the customer relationship.  
Cortex builds the solution.  
You deliver it.**
