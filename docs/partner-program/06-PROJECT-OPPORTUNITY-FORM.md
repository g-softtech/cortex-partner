# Cortex Partner Program — Project Opportunity Submission

**Version:** 1.0  
**Route:** `/projects/new`  
**Audience:** Approved Partners

## Purpose

This form is used when a Partner identifies a potential customer who needs a software or digital solution.

The goal is to give Cortex enough information to assess the opportunity.

This is NOT the full development kickoff.

## Information to Collect

### Project Type

Examples:

- Business website
- Web application
- Business management system
- E-commerce solution
- Custom software
- Digital platform
- Other approved digital solution

### Customer Need

- What does the customer need?
- What problem are they trying to solve?

### Main Requirements

- Main features requested
- Important functionality
- Any known integrations
- Other relevant requirements

### Commercial Information

- Customer's expected budget, if known
- Desired timeline, if known

### Additional Information

- Anything else Cortex should know to assess the opportunity

## Customer Information

Do not unnecessarily require detailed customer contact information at this stage.

The form exists primarily for opportunity assessment.

Detailed development information is collected after the project is won.

## Submission Result

After submission, the project enters the Cortex assessment workflow.

Possible early-stage statuses may include:

- SUBMITTED
- UNDER_REVIEW
- PRICED
- PROPOSAL_SENT
- WON
- DECLINED

The exact platform status values must follow the existing production schema.
